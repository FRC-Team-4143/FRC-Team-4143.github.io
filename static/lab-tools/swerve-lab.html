Things to try
  1. Spin in place with only the right stick. Every wheel shows the same speed: ω × 0.269 m.
  2. Full forward plus full spin, then turn off Desaturate. The path bends.
  3. Turn off Optimize, set steering to 4 rad/s and flick from full forward to full back.
  4. Turn off Optimize and Cosine scaling, then reverse fast. The robot lurches the wrong way first.
  5. Turn off Discretize and drive straight while spinning hard in field-centric. The path drifts.
  6. In Rotation lock, strafe around the hub. The right stick does nothing.

Which fixes we use. MWLib's Module.runSetpoint calls optimize and cosineScale on every module, and every drive request calls ChassisSpeeds.discretize. The drive requests do not call desaturateWheelSpeeds: in open-loop teleop a wheel asked for more than SPEED_AT_12V_MPS just runs at 12 V. Turn Desaturate off to see that.
