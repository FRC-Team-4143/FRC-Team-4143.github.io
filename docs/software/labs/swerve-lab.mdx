# Swerve Kinematics Lab
Drive a swerve robot with two joysticks and watch the math: the sticks become one `ChassisSpeeds`, kinematics turns it into a speed and angle for each of the four wheels, and the wheels move the robot. Compare field-centric and robot-centric driving, and see desaturation and module optimization at work.
