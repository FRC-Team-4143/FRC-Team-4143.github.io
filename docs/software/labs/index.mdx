# 🧪 Labs
The labs let you try the ideas from these docs on a simulated robot, right in your browser. There is nothing to install
and nothing to break. Each lab has a guided **Walkthrough** if you are new to the topic, and a **Things to try** list
once you know your way around.

Every lab is on its own page in this section, and also full screen from the **Labs** tab at the top of the site.

<LabList />

The PID Tuning and Pose Estimator labs are adapted, with thanks, from the labs by
[FRC Team 1756 Argos](https://github.com/FRC1756-Argos/teamargos.org). The Behavior Tree Lab draws trees with Arborist,
the viewer from [Arbor](https://github.com/coalman321/Arbor).
