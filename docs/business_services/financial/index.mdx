### The Finance Problem
The coach says: *"We have \$8,000 in the bank. Can we afford our next competition?"*

To answer, we need to know:
- How much cash is available?
- What bills do we owe?
- What have we already spent?
- What expenses are coming?
- How much money is expected?
- What equipment do we own?

This is where bookkeeping helps.
