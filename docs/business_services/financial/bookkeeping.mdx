# What Is Bookkeeping?
Bookkeeping is the process of:
- Recording transactions
- Organizing financial information
- Tracking money
- Maintaining accurate records

**Bookkeeping tells the financial story.**

## Example
Starting with \$10,000, during one month the team:
- Receives \$2,000 from a sponsor
- Spends \$800 on robot parts
- Pays \$600 for competition registration
- Raises \$500 through fundraising
- Buys \$300 of supplies

The team ends the month with **\$10,800**. But does \$10,800 tell us everything? What if the team still owes a parts
supplier \$1,000, or has a \$2,000 hotel bill coming next week?

:::tip Key Lesson
**Cash is not the whole story.**
:::
