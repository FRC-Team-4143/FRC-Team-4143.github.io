# What Is Bookkeeping?
Bookkeeping is the process of:
- Recording transactions
- Organizing financial information
- Tracking money
- Maintaining accurate records

**Bookkeeping tells the financial story.**

## Cash Is Not the Whole Story
Imagine that during one month the team receives a sponsorship, buys robot parts, pays a competition registration
fee, and holds a fundraiser. Looking at the bank account at the end of the month tells you how much cash is there, but
not everything you need to know. What if the team still owes a parts supplier, or has a hotel bill coming next week?

:::tip Key Lesson
**Cash is not the whole story.** Good records show what we have, what we owe, and what is coming.
:::
