# The Accounting Equation
**Assets = Liabilities + Fund Balance**

Think: *what we have = what we owe + what we have left.*

If the team owns equipment and has cash in the bank, but also owes a supplier for parts, the fund balance is what is
left after subtracting what we owe from what we have.

## Team Challenge
The team has cash in the bank and owns some equipment, and it has an unpaid bill from a supplier. Which items are
assets, which are liabilities, and how do you find the fund balance?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- **Assets:** the cash and the equipment
- **Liabilities:** the unpaid supplier bill
- **Fund Balance** = assets - liabilities (cash + equipment - the unpaid bill)

</details>
