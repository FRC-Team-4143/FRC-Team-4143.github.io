# Recording Transactions
We use **double entry accounting**: every financial transaction has two sides. Whatever happens on one side of the
books must have an equal and opposite entry somewhere else. Each entry is either a **debit** or a **credit**.

## Exercise
For each transaction, which account is debited and which is credited?
- A donation is received
- Robot supplies are purchased
- A fundraiser brings in income
- A team member is reimbursed for a Saturday lunch

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

| Transaction | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Donation received | Cash | Fundraising |
| Robot supplies purchased | Supplies | Cash |
| Fundraiser income | Cash | Fundraising |
| Saturday lunch reimbursement | Meals | Cash |

Money coming into the bank is a **debit** to Cash. Money going out is a **credit** to Cash, with the matching debit
going to the expense it paid for.

</details>
