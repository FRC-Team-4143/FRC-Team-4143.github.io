# The Five Basic Account Types
| Account Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Assets** | What we have | Cash, bank account, robot, tools, 3D printer, computers, supplies, money owed to us |
| **Liabilities** | What we owe | Unpaid parts bills, credit card balances, unpaid registration, hotel bills, vendor invoices |
| **Fund Balance** | Our accumulated financial resources | What is left after considering what the team owes |
| **Revenue** | Money coming in | Sponsor contributions, fundraising, donations, grants, team fees, school support |
| **Expenses** | Costs of running the team | Robot parts, competition fees, travel, meals, supplies, team shirts, software |

## Fund Balance
Fund balance is the team's financial resources after considering what the team owes. Instead of asking *"Did we make
a profit?"*, we ask *"Did our available funds increase or decrease?"*

## Sorting Challenge
What type of account is each item?
1. Money in the team bank account
2. A bill owed to a parts supplier
3. A sponsor contribution
4. Money spent on motors
5. A 3D printer
6. Fundraising income

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Asset
2. Liability
3. Revenue
4. Expense
5. Asset
6. Revenue

</details>
