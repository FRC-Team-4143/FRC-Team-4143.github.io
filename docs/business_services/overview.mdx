# Business Services

Welcome to the Business Services documentation for the MARS/WARS robotics team! Business Services is the part of the
team that keeps everything else running: we tell the team's story, connect with our community, and secure the funding
that makes each season possible.

## Why Business Services Matters
- We represent MARS/WARS in our local community, the *FIRST* community, and anywhere the team has an online presence.
- We secure the sponsors, donors, and partnerships that sustain our team's success.
- We create meaningful connections that grow our impact on students and our community.

## What We Do
Business Services work falls into three areas: **Team**, **Community**, and **Funding**.

### Team
How the team presents itself, inside and out.
- Merchandise
- Social media
- Website
- Photography and videography
- Graphic design
- Reveal videos
- Anything else the team needs to look its best

### Community
We plan and run the team's outreach and events. That means we:
- Plan and run events
- Schedule and coordinate students and mentors
- Design event flyers, signs, and promotional materials
- Promote events through social media
- Organize event logistics and supplies

Events we support include:
- MARS/SPARKS
- Volunteering
- Open House
- Craft and Vendor Fair
- Fundraising events
- Recruitment events

### Funding
**Why is funding so important?** On average, a typical season costs the team **\$60,000 to \$70,000**.

**How do we secure funding?**
- Sponsors
- Fundraising
- Local donations

**How do we track our finances?** This year we are introducing budgeting to Business Services. We keep track of how
much money we bring in, how much we spend, and where it goes. The [Financial](./financial/index.mdx) docs cover how
this works.
