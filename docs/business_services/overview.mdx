# Business Services

Business Services runs the non-robot side of MARS/WARS: how the team presents itself, its outreach and events, and the
funding that makes each season possible. We represent the team in our local community, in the *FIRST* community, and
online.

## What We Do
| Area | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **Team** | Merchandise, social media, website, photography, videography, graphic design, reveal videos |
| **Community** | Planning, staffing, and promoting events such as MARS/SPARKS, volunteering, Open House, vendor fairs, fundraisers, and recruitment events |
| **Funding** | Sponsors, fundraising, donations, and tracking where the money goes |
