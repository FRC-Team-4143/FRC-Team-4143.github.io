# Branding
Consistent branding shows professionalism and makes MARS/WARS recognizable. Anything that represents the team, whether
it is a social media post, flyer, presentation, shirt, or website, should follow these standards.

:::tip
Everything on this page lives in the **MARS/WARS Brand Kit** in the team Canva, under the **Brand** tab. When in doubt,
use what is in the Brand tab.
:::

---

## The Name
### MARS/WARS
Our organization name is **MARS/WARS**, which is treated as an acronym:
- All caps
- No spaces
- A slash in between

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|------|---------|
| MARS/WARS | Mars/Wars, MARS WARS, Mars Wars, MW |

### MARS' Minions
Our JV team is **MARS' Minions**, which is **not** an acronym:
- Only "MARS'" and the "M" in "Minions" are capitalized
- The apostrophe comes after the "S" in MARS
- No slash in between

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|------|---------|
| MARS' Minions | MAR'S Minions, MARS'/Minions, Mars' Minions, MARS' MINIONS |

## Design Standards
- **Use the team Canva.** Make team designs in the team Canva account so you have the Brand Kit, and share every team
  design within the team.
- **Social media format.** Most of our flyers and promotional media are posted on social media, so use the
  **Instagram Post (4:5)** design size unless something else is needed.
- **Templates are inspiration only.** You can look at Canva templates for ideas, but do not post a template as a final
  design.
- **No AI-generated designs.** We never post a post or flyer created by AI. Canva AI can help you get started, but the
  final design is yours. See [Using AI](./tools/using-ai.mdx).
- **Personal designs stay personal.** You can use your team-linked Canva account for personal or school projects, but
  don't share those designs with the team.

---

## Writing Standards
- **Write like a person.** If you use AI to help write, read it over and make sure it doesn't sound like a robot.
- **Check the name.** Before you post, make sure MARS/WARS and MARS' Minions are written correctly.
- **Proofread.** Check spelling and grammar before anything goes out under the team's name.
