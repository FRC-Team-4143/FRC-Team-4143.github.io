# Branding
Anything that represents the team (posts, flyers, presentations, apparel, the website) follows these standards. The
logos, colors, and fonts all live in the **MARS/WARS Brand Kit** under the **Brand** tab in the team Canva. If
something here and the Brand Kit ever disagree, the Brand Kit wins.

## The Name
| Team | Number | Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|---|---|
| MARS/WARS | 4143 | **MARS/WARS** (all caps, no spaces, slash) | Mars/Wars, MARS WARS, Mars Wars, MW |
| MARS' Minions | 4423 | **MARS' Minions** (apostrophe after the S, no slash) | MAR'S Minions, MARS'/Minions, Mars' Minions, MARS' MINIONS |

## Fonts
| Use | Fonts |
|-----|-------|
| Titles and headlines | Impact, Norwester |
| Body text | TT Fors, Arial |

Other fonts are fine when a design's theme calls for it.

## Design Standards
- Make team designs in the team Canva and share them with the team.
- Use the **Instagram Post (4:5)** size for flyers and promo graphics unless there's a reason not to.
- Templates are for inspiration, not final designs.
- No AI-generated posts or flyers. See [Using AI](./tools/using-ai.mdx).
- Proofread everything, and double-check the team name, before it goes out.
