# Using AI
AI is a useful tool when it's used well. We use it to brainstorm, improve writing, and get a starting point for designs.

## Brainstorming
Good for getting past a blank page: kickoff activities, post ideas and captions, themes, robot names, and video
concepts.

## Writing
AI can fix spelling and grammar, tighten or expand text, adjust tone, or turn bullet points into a paragraph. Always
edit what it gives you: it doesn't know the team, and it tends to sound robotic. Add context or your own words until
it sounds like us.

## Designs
:::danger
We never post an AI-generated post or flyer.
:::

Canva AI is fine as a starting point, but the final design should be yours. Designs from tools like ChatGPT are easy
to spot.
