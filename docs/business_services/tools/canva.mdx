# Canva
Canva is our main tool for visual content: social media posts, flyers, banners, signs, printed material, event
presentations, merchandise, and photo and video editing.

## Setup
1. Sign up with your school or personal email.
2. Ask a Business Services mentor for an invite to the team Canva. The team account includes Canva Premium, shared
   designs, and the [Brand Kit](../branding.mdx).
3. Confirm the MARS/WARS logo shows in the bottom left corner, so you know you're working in the team account.

Share every team design within the team. You're welcome to use the team account for personal or school projects, but
keep those out of the team's shared designs.

## Learning Canva
Canva's **Canva Essentials** course (Home ⟶ **...** ⟶ **Design School**) covers the basics with short videos and
activities.
