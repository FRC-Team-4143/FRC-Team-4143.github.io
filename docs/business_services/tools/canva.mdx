# Canva
Canva is our primary tool to create, manage, and collaborate on visual content. We use it to:
- Design social media posts (Facebook and Instagram)
- Create flyers, banners, signs, and printed material
- Create and present presentations for Open House and Trivia Night
- Design merchandise
- Edit pictures
- Edit videos

---

## Setting Up Canva
1. Sign up or log in using either your school or personal email account.
2. Use the code sent in the **Business Services** channel on Slack to join the team's Canva. This gives you:
    - Access to Canva Premium
    - Easy sharing of designs
    - The ability to collaborate with team members on designs
3. Make sure you see the MARS/WARS meatball logo in the bottom left corner.

:::note
Whenever you make a design for the team, make sure you share it within the team.
:::

## The MARS/WARS Brand
Everything about our brand is in the **Brand** tab in Canva: logos, colors, and fonts. Read the
[Branding](../branding.mdx) page for the team's brand standards, including how to write the team name, logo rules,
our colors, and fonts.

## Canva Design School
New members learn the basics with Canva's own **Canva Essentials** course.
1. On the Home page, click the **...** on the left side of the screen.
2. Click **Design School**.
3. In the search bar, type "Canva Essentials".
4. Select the **Canva Essentials** course.

The course has videos and activities. In training we watch the videos together and then complete each video's
activity on our own.

---

## Tips
- Feel free to use your team-linked Canva account for personal or school projects to get more comfortable with Canva.
  Just don't share those designs with the team.
- Look out for the **Canva Tip of the Day** in our meetings to learn skills we might not normally think to use.
