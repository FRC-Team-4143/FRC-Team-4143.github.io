# Canva
Canva is our primary tool to create, manage, and collaborate on visual content. We use it to:
- Design social media posts (Facebook and Instagram)
- Create flyers, banners, signs, and printed material
- Create and present presentations for Open House and Trivia Night
- Design merchandise
- Edit pictures
- Edit videos

---

## Setting Up Canva
1. Sign up or log in using either your school or personal email account.
2. Use the code sent in the **Business Services** channel on Slack to join the team's Canva. This gives you:
    - Access to Canva Premium
    - Easy sharing of designs
    - The ability to collaborate with team members on designs
3. Make sure you see the MARS/WARS meatball logo in the bottom left corner.

:::note
Whenever you make a design for the team, make sure you share it within the team.
:::

## The MARS/WARS Brand
Everything about our brand is in the **Brand** tab in Canva:
- **Logos:** 4143 and 4423, *FIRST*, and the game logo (updated each year)
- **Colors:** Team, *FIRST*, and the game logo colors (updated each year)
- **Fonts**

Consistent branding shows professionalism and is important in making our organization recognizable.

### The Name
Our organization name is **MARS/WARS**, which is treated as an acronym:
- All caps
- No spaces
- A slash in between

This is how the name should appear on any media content, presentation, or other material.

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|------|---------|
| MARS/WARS | Mars/Wars, MARS WARS, Mars Wars, MW |

Our JV team is **MARS' Minions**, which is **not** an acronym:
- Only "MARS'" and the "M" in "Minions" are capitalized
- The apostrophe comes after the "S" in MARS
- No slash in between

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|------|---------|
| MARS' Minions | MAR'S Minions, MARS'/Minions, Mars' Minions, MARS' MINIONS |

### Fonts
We have selected fonts we commonly use for graphic design. You can find them in Canva under **Brand ⟶ Fonts**. We
recommend using these fonts, but sometimes another font fits a design's theme better.
- **Titles and headlines:** Impact or Norwester
- **Body text:** TT Fors or Arial

## Canva Training
We use Canva's own **Canva Essentials** course to learn the basics.
1. On the Home page, click the **...** on the left side of the screen.
2. Click **Design School**.
3. In the search bar, type "Canva Essentials".
4. Select the **Canva Essentials** course.

The course has videos and activities. We watch the videos together and then complete each video's activity on our
own.

## Canva Practice
Put your new skills to the test by creating 3 designs:
1. **An FRC 2027 Kickoff flyer.** This is not a real public event, but treat it like one for this exercise. Your flyer
must include:
    - Name of the event: FRC 2027 Kickoff
    - Date and time: January 9th, 2027 at 10:00am
    - Location: Shoff Field
    - What the event is, in your own words, including the time of the official kickoff video (12:00pm). If you are new
      to the team and are not sure what kickoff is, ask for help!
    - That the event will include food
    - Who is hosting: MARS/WARS
    - The MARS/WARS logo (optionally the *FIRST*, CANOPY, or BIOCORE logos)
    - Format: use the **Instagram Post (4:5)** design, since most of our flyers and promotional media are posted on
      social media
2. **A poster that resembles you.** You may look at templates for inspiration, but do not use one as your final design.
3. **A business card for an imaginary company.** You may look at templates for inspiration, but do not use one as your
final design.

We will share and talk about these designs so we can learn from each other.

## Wrap Up
- We discuss everyone's designs at the next meeting, so if you have not finished, work on them outside of meeting
  times.
- To get more comfortable with Canva, feel free to use your team-linked Canva account for personal or educational use.
  Just don't share those designs with the team.
- We include a **Canva Tip of the Day** in our meetings to learn skills we might not normally think to use.
