# Using AI
AI can be incredibly useful if you know how to use it appropriately. Business Services uses AI to:
- Brainstorm ideas
- Improve and proofread writing
- Get a starting point for designs

These training presentations were created with the help of AI.

---

## Brainstorming Ideas
Sometimes the hardest part is coming up with the first idea. If you are stuck, turn to AI to get the ball moving and
inspire ideas you can build off of. For example:
- **Kickoff ideas:** activities and games
- **Social media:** post ideas and captions
- **Themes:** robot names, designs, and videos

---

## Writing With AI
As a team we do a lot of writing, whether it is a social media post, our website, or a Slack message to the team. AI
can help you:
- Fix spelling and grammar
- Make the text clearer or more professional
- Shorten or extend the text
- Set a tone that fits the message
- Turn bullet points into a polished paragraph

### AI Is a Robot
AI is a wonderful tool for improving our writing, but **always read over what it gives you**.
- Sometimes AI writes text that sounds like a robot, and we want to avoid that. You can prompt it to change the tone
  to sound more like a high school student.
- AI doesn't know everything about our team or what we are trying to say. Either give it more information on the
  topic, or take what it gave you and add your own words.

## Designing With AI
AI can be helpful as a starting point for designs.

:::danger
We will **never** post an AI-created post or flyer.
:::

Even if you think a design doesn't look like AI, there are several signs that give it away. You can use Canva AI to
help create your designs, but a design made with ChatGPT will be obvious.

---

## AI Practice
Pick a past FRC game and use AI to help create a complete creative concept for the team. You are not using AI to do the
project for you: use it to brainstorm and guide you along the way.

Your concept needs:
1. An overall theme for the team (for example, Jumanji or Back to the Future)
2. A name for the robot
3. Ideas for a fun shirt design
4. An idea for a teaser video
5. A short paragraph about preseason so far and the team getting ready for the new season, written in your theme (not
   the Jumanji theme!)
