# Financial
**Team Bookkeeping: how to keep the money working while you build the robot.**

In this session, **you are the team treasurer!** By the end you should understand:
- Why bookkeeping matters to a robotics team
- Assets, liabilities, revenue, expenses, and fund balance
- The accounting equation
- Debits and credits
- Journal entries
- How to record common robotics team transactions

- 🚐 Transportation
- 🏨 Hotels
- 👕 Team apparel
- 📣 Marketing
- 🍕 Meals

### The Finance Problem
The coach says: *"We have \$8,000 in the bank. Can we afford our next competition?"*

To answer, we need to know:
- How much cash is available?
- What bills do we owe?
- What have we already spent?
- What expenses are coming?
- How much money is expected?
- What equipment do we own?

This is where bookkeeping helps.

---

## What Is Bookkeeping?
Bookkeeping is the process of:
- Recording transactions
- Organizing financial information
- Tracking money
- Maintaining accurate records

**Bookkeeping tells the financial story.**

### Example
Starting with \$10,000, during one month the team:
- Receives \$2,000 from a sponsor
- Spends \$800 on robot parts
- Pays \$600 for competition registration
- Raises \$500 through fundraising
- Buys \$300 of supplies

The team ends the month with **\$10,800**. But does \$10,800 tell us everything? What if the team still owes a parts
supplier \$1,000, or has a \$2,000 hotel bill coming next week?

:::tip Key Lesson
**Cash is not the whole story.**
:::

## The Five Basic Account Types
| Account Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Assets** | What we have | Cash, bank account, robot, tools, 3D printer, computers, supplies, money owed to us |
| **Liabilities** | What we owe | Unpaid parts bills, credit card balances, unpaid registration, hotel bills, vendor invoices |
| **Fund Balance** | Our accumulated financial resources | What is left after considering what the team owes |
| **Revenue** | Money coming in | Sponsor contributions, fundraising, donations, grants, team fees, school support |
| **Expenses** | Costs of running the team | Robot parts, competition fees, travel, meals, supplies, team shirts, software |

### Fund Balance
Fund balance is the team's financial resources after considering what the team owes. Instead of asking *"Did we make
a profit?"*, we ask *"Did our available funds increase or decrease?"*

### Sorting Challenge
What type of account is each item?
1. \$5,000 in the team bank account
2. \$600 owed to a parts supplier
3. \$2,000 sponsor contribution
4. \$900 spent on motors
5. \$3,000 3D printer
6. \$750 fundraising income

<details>
<summary>Answers</summary>

1. Asset
2. Liability
3. Revenue
4. Expense
5. Asset
6. Revenue

</details>

## The Accounting Equation
**Assets = Liabilities + Fund Balance**

Think: *what we have = what we owe + what we have left.*

For example, if the team has \$6,000 cash, \$2,000 of equipment, and owes \$1,000 in bills:
- Assets: \$8,000
- Liabilities: \$1,000
- Fund Balance: \$7,000

### Team Challenge
The team has \$4,000 cash, \$2,000 of equipment, and owes a supplier \$500. What is the fund balance?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

- Assets: \$4,000 cash + \$2,000 equipment
- Liabilities: \$500 owed to a supplier
- Fund Balance = **\$5,500** (\$4,000 + \$2,000 - \$500)

</details>

## Recording Transactions
We use **double entry accounting**: every financial transaction has two sides. Whatever happens on one side of the
books must have an equal and opposite entry somewhere else. Each entry is either a **debit** or a **credit**.

## MARS/WARS Financial Tracking
Every transaction is recorded in the team's financial tracker with a **category** and a **subcategory**.

### Categories
| Income | Expense |
|---|---|
| Fundraising | Competition |
| Regrant | Marketing |
| Student Fees | Outreach |
| Misc Income | Supplies |
| | Misc Expenses |

### Subcategories
| Category | Subcategories |
|---|---|
| **Fundraising** | Donations, Pork Chop Lunch, Sponsor, Super Bowl Squares, Trivia, Vendor Fair, Misc |
| **Competition** | Fees, Hotel, Meals, Misc |
| **Marketing** | Fun Shirts, Swag, Uniforms, Misc |
| **Outreach** | Lego League, Misc |
| **Supplies** | Field, Robot, Shop |

Subcategories are written as `Category - Subcategory`, for example `Fundraising - Trivia` or `Competition - Hotel`.

### Tracker Columns
| Column | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Date** | The transaction date (not the date you are recording it) |
| **Amount** | Income is a positive number, expenses are negative |
| **Cash/Check/CC/Shopify** | How the money was spent or received |
| **Vendor** | The vendor, for expenses |
| **Check Number** | If the team wrote an actual check, the check number for tracking |
| **Cleared** | Put an "X" here once the check has cleared the bank |
| **Account** | The team has 2 accounts: a NFP (Not for Profit) account and a school account |
| **Team** | If an expense can be assigned to a specific team, record it here; otherwise leave it blank |
| **Income/Expense** | Fills in automatically based on the amount you enter |
| **Category** | The high level income or expense category |
| **Subcategory** | The lower level category used for financial reporting |
| **Notes** | Any important notes |
