# Business Services Overview
In this training module, we meet the Business Services group and cover what we do for the team: how MARS/WARS presents
itself, the community events we run, and how we fund each season.

---

## Topics
- Why Business Services matters
- **Team:** merchandise, social media, the website, photography, videography, and graphic design
- **Community:** planning and promoting outreach events
- **Funding:** sponsors, fundraising, donations, and budgeting
