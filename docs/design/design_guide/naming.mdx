Giving parts descriptive names helps your fellow designers find parts quickly and avoid duplicate work.

## Onshape Parts

<span style={{color: 'red'}}>Year</span>-<span style={{color: 'dodgerblue'}}>Robot_Code</span>-<span style={{color: 'green'}}>Subsystem_Code</span>-<span style={{color: 'mediumpurple'}}>Part_Number</span>

**Example:**

- 2025-A **(Robot)**
    - 2025-A-IN **(Intake Subsystem)**
        - 2025-A-IN-01
        - 2025-A-IN-02
        - 2025-A-IN-03
        - 2025-A-IN-04
    - 2025-A-AR **(Arm Subsystem)**
        - 2025-A-AR-01
        - 2025-A-AR-02
        - 2025-A-AR-03
        - 2025-A-AR-04
    - **etc.**

## Fusion Parts

```text
Year-Robot_Code-Subsystem_Code-Part_Number_Date_Downloaded_Comments
```

**Example:**

```text
2025-A-AR-01_10-01-2025_Initial-Design
2025-A-AR-01_10-01-2025_Updated-Design
2025-A-AR-01_10-02-2025_Fixed-Holes
```

## G-Code

```text
Year-Robot_Code-Subsystem_Code-Part_Number-Date_Generated-Comments
```
