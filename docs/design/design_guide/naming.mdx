Giving parts descriptive names help your fellow designers to quickly and efficiently find parts and avoid repeat efforts.

### Onshape Parts

<font color="red">Year</font>-<font color="blue">Robot_Code</font>-<font color="green">Subsystem_Code</font>-<font color="purple">Part_Number</font>

**Example:**


- 2025-A    **(Robot)**
    - 2025-A-IN     **(Intake Subsystem)**
        - 2025-A-IN-01
        - 2025-A-IN-02
        - 2025-A-IN-03
        - 2025-A-IN-04
    - 2025-A-AR     **(Arm Subsystem)**
        - 2025-A-AR-01
        - 2025-A-AR-02
        - 2025-A-AR-03
        - 2025-A-AR-04 
    - **etc**
