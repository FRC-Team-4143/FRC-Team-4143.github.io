Common stock materials and hardware used on our robots, with guidance on where each is appropriate.

## Stock Materials

### Polycarbonate Sheet

| Thickness | Usage |
|---|---|
| 1/8" | Low stress, non-structural areas where direct impact is unlikely. |
| 1/4" | Medium stress areas with some structural use where direct impact is possible. |

Polycarbonate is strong, but flexes much more than aluminum without breaking, and it can be bent. Its density is about
half of aluminum's. Typical uses:

- **1/8":** indexer and feeder sides, shooter sides, guards.
- **1/4":** intakes and elevator supports.

### Aluminum Sheet

Plates up to **8" wide** fit in the CNC mill.

| Thickness | Usage |
|---|---|
| 1/8" | Gussets, motor mounts, shooter sides. |
| 1/4" | High load parts (elevator mounts, gearbox sides, climber parts). Pocket to save weight. |

### Aluminum Rectangular Tubing

⭐ marks our preferred sizes, which are typically in stock. Other sizes may be available as small pieces, but
usually need to be ordered. All tubing is 6061 aluminum.

| Dimensions | Wall Thickness | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| 1" x 2" ⭐ | 1/16" | Most structural parts. |
| 1" x 1" ⭐ | 1/16" | Most structural parts. |
| 1" x 2" or 1" x 3" | 1/8" | Frame rails. |
| 2" x 3" | 1/8" | Higher load-bearing structures, chassis components. |
| 1.5" x 1.5", 2" x 2" square | | Telescoping mechanisms. |

1/8" wall aluminum angle is also available.

## Fasteners

### Bolts

⭐ marks our most commonly used size. If possible, design for **10-32** (32 threads per inch) button head or socket
head cap screws. We stock various lengths. Some COTS components require other bolt sizes.

When modeling, think through assembly: can the bolt actually be installed, and is there clearance for wrenches?

## Bearings and Shafts

Bearings support spinning parts. They need to be installed in material that is at least **0.125" thick**.

| Bearing | OD | ID / Shaft |
|---|---|---|
| FR8 or R8 | 1.125" | 0.5" round |
| 1/2" hex flange | 1.125" | 1/2" hex |
| 1/2" ThunderHex flange | 1.125" | 1/2" ThunderHex |
| FR6 or R6 | 0.875" | 0.375" round |
| 3/8" ThunderHex flange | 0.875" | 3/8" ThunderHex |
