An important aspect of every FRC season is taking possession of game pieces efficiently and reliably. The intake mechanism is responsible for grabbing these game pieces from the field and delivering them to other subsystems, such as shooters or manipulators. A well-designed intake must be able to quickly and consistently gain control of the game piece.

When designing an intake mechanism, consider the following key factors:

1. (Size, Shape, Possible Orientations of Game Piece?)
2. (END orientation?)          
    Something about reducing output orientation options.
    Something about avoid areas where the game piece can get stuck.
3. (How to chain the game piece into the next subsystem?)
