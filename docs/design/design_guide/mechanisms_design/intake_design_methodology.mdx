An important part of every FRC season is taking possession of game pieces efficiently and reliably. The intake is
responsible for grabbing game pieces from the field and delivering them to other subsystems, such as shooters or
manipulators. A well-designed intake must quickly and consistently gain control of the game piece.

## Key Questions

When designing an intake, consider the following:

1. **What are the game piece's size, shape, and possible orientations?**
2. **What orientation should the game piece end up in?**
    - Reduce the number of possible output orientations.
    - Avoid areas where the game piece can get stuck.
3. **How does the game piece get handed off to the next subsystem?**

## Design Tips

- Maximize the intake area.
- Roller surface speed should be at least 2x the robot's max speed.
- Grip
    - Normal forces
    - Rigid parts with compliant wheels, or compliant parts with rigid wheels.
- Build it robust.
- Sensors
    - Place one close to the intake point, where the game piece is first in possession.
    - Place one at any staging location.
