In this training module, we will cover how to take a part from Onshape to a program our CNC machines can run: picking
tools, creating a setup and operations in Fusion, posting the program, and running it safely.

We have three CNC machines: a **Tormach PCNC 1100 mill** (aluminum, wood, and smaller pieces of polycarbonate), a
**FANUC CNC 40 taper mill** at McBride & Shoff (automatic tool changer, can tap threads), and a **Shapeoko 5 router**
in the woodshop (larger pieces of wood and polycarbonate, **not aluminum**).

:::danger Safety first
Read [Shop Safety](../mechanical/machining/safety.mdx) before working on any machine.
:::

## Exercises

- Model the practice part in Onshape, make a drawing of it, and print the drawing.
- Export the part as a STEP file and open it in Fusion.
- Create a setup with the right stock, WCS offset, and program comment.
- Add drill and bore operations, then post process the setup to an `.nc` file.
- Go through the [pre-run checklist](../mechanical/cam/start.mdx#pre-run-checklist) with a mentor before running it.
