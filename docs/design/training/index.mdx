Preseason design training gets every design team member ready to model, manufacture, and print robot parts before
build season starts. Everyone starts with Onshape training on FRCDesign, then attends the seminars below.

## Onshape Training

Work through the [FRCDesign learning course](./onshape) at your own pace. What you need to finish depends on whether
you are new to the team.

| Who | What to complete |
|---|---|
| 1st year team members | All of **Stage 1** |
| Returning team members | 1 or 2 projects from **Stage 2** |
| Everyone | Robot projects |
