# 📚 Training Materials

Welcome to the MARS/WARS design pre-season training! It gets new and returning design team members ready to model,
manufacture, and print robot parts before build season starts. Everyone starts with Onshape training on FRCDesign,
then attends the seminars below.

:::note
Each seminar page has the presentation, hands-on exercises, and the written notes from the seminar, so you can catch
up on anything you missed.
:::

## Onshape Training

Work through the [FRCDesign learning course](./onshape.mdx) at your own pace. What you need to finish depends on whether
you are new to the team.

| Who | What to complete |
|---|---|
| 1st year team members | All of **Stage 1** |
| Returning team members | 1 or 2 projects from **Stage 2** |
| Everyone | Robot projects |
