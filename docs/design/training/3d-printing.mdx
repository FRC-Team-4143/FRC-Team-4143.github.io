printers: exporting an STL, choosing a printer, orienting the part and deciding on supports, then slicing and printing.

## Exercises

- Export a part you modeled in Onshape as an STL file.
- Load it into Bambu Studio, choose a printer, orient it, and decide whether it needs supports.
- Slice it, check the print plate for warnings or errors, and print it.
    - For an extra challenge, fillet every edge, then print two parts on one plate.
