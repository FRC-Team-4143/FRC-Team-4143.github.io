## Exercises

- Download a part from McMaster-Carr as a STEP file and import it into Onshape.
- Insert a COTS part (like a wheel or motor) from the FRCDesign Inserter into an assembly.
- Use ReCalc to size a motor and gear ratio for an arm, and paste the link into a comment in your Onshape model.
- Pick a part from a past robot and answer the
  [questions to ask before modeling a new part](../design_guide/onshape.mdx#before-modeling-a-new-part).
