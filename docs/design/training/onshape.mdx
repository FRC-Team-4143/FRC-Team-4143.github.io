We design our robots in [Onshape](https://www.onshape.com/). To learn it, we use the free
[FRCDesign learning course](https://frcdesign.org/learning-course/), which teaches Onshape and FRC-specific design
practices through short lessons and hands-on exercises.

## What to Complete

### 1st Year Team Members

Complete **all of Stage 1**. Stage 1 covers the Onshape fundamentals we use on every robot, including deriving
sketches from a master sketch (unit 1D).

### Returning Team Members

Complete **1 or 2 projects from Stage 2**. Pick projects that stretch a skill you haven't used much yet. If you aren't
sure which to pick, ask a design lead.

### Everyone: Robot Projects

After your stage work, everyone moves on to robot projects: designing real mechanisms using the
[Design Guide](../design_guide/materials) and the practices from the
[Design Guide & Suppliers seminar](./design-guide-suppliers).
