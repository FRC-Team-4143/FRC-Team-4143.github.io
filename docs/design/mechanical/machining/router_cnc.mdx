# CNC Router

Computer numerical control (CNC) Router is a form of [milling](./milling_manual) with an emphasis on 2D shapes.


## CNC Safety

A CNC Router is a dangerous piece of industrial equipment and safety must be considered during operation.  
:::danger

The dangers include

- ***High Rotational Energy***  
    A **moderate risk** in CNC Router during operations and setup.  
    This can cause lacerations.  
    This is generally caused by **contact with the tooling**.  
    To reduce the risk of harm, utilize a brush to move any material from around the tool and do not use your hands.
    When dealing with large cutouts that have a chance of jamming the spindle utilize a wooden plank to remove debris or hold down material.  

- ***Flying Debris***  
    A **minor risk** in CNC Router operations is being struck by debris.  
    This debris can be metal/plastic/wood shavings, objects that have been parted off, or shattered tooling.  
    This can cause minor lacerations and eye injury.  
    To reduce the risk of harm, always **wear safety glasses** while in proximity of an operational CNC Router, and confirm the shield is in place.

- ***Sharp Edges***  
    A **minor risk** in CNC Router operations in being cut by sharp edges.  
    Objects like metal shavings, cutting surfaces, and machined edges have sharp edges.  
    This can cause minor lacerations.  
    To avoid being hurt do not handle metal shavings with bare hands, utilize cleaning tools and gloves.  
    To avoid being hurt machined parts should be sanded/filed to remove sharp edges. 

    :::note
    **Gloves** should never be worn during CNC Router operation due to risk of entanglement.  
    Only when the spindle has come to a complete stop is it ok to wear gloves and handle parts.
:::

## CNC Router Operation

### Machine Bootup
**Initialize the machine.**
1. Lift Router spindle above the bed.
2. Twist **E-Stop** to switched Router ON.
3. Power up connect PC.
4. Login as LanClub.
5. Launch Carbide Motion.
6. Press Initialize Machine.
    - Machine will home again the back right corner.
    - Machine will request to calibrate tooling height.


### Program Setup
To begin CNC setup first you must import a file from a flash drive prepared by the fusion programmer.  

1. Click on
2. Navigate to the desired g-code file on the right side.
3. Open the desired file.
4. Click on **Top View** and view part orientation and origin.
5. Navigate to the **Jog** menu.
6. Position the spindle above the desired origin point.
7. Click on **Set Zero X & Y** and confirm the origin point is set.
    - Z Zero must only be set if you believe the height is off.
