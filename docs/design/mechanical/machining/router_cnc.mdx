Computer numerical control (CNC) routing is a form of [milling](./milling_manual) with an emphasis on cutting 2D shapes
out of sheet material.

## CNC Router Safety

A CNC router is a dangerous piece of industrial equipment, and safety must be considered during operation. Review the
general [shop safety rules](./safety) first.

:::danger Hazards

**High Rotational Energy** (moderate risk)
- **Harm:** Lacerations.
- **Cause:** Contact with the tooling.
- **Prevention:** Use a brush, not your hands, to move material away from the tool. For large cutouts that could jam the
  spindle, use a wooden plank to clear debris or hold down material.

**Flying Debris** (minor risk)
- **Harm:** Minor lacerations and eye injury from metal, plastic, or wood shavings, parted-off pieces, or shattered
  tooling.
- **Prevention:** Always **wear safety glasses** near an operating CNC router, and confirm the shield is in place.

**Sharp Edges** (minor risk)
- **Harm:** Minor lacerations from shavings, cutting surfaces, and machined edges.
- **Prevention:** Never handle shavings with bare hands; use cleaning tools and gloves. Sand or file machined parts to
  remove sharp edges.

:::

:::warning Gloves
**Gloves must never be worn while the CNC router is running** due to the risk of entanglement. Only put on gloves to
handle parts once the spindle has come to a complete stop.
:::

## Machine Startup

1. Raise the router spindle above the bed.
2. Twist the **E-Stop** to switch the router ON.
3. Power up the connected PC.
4. Log in as **LanClub**.
5. Launch **Carbide Motion**.
6. Press **Initialize Machine**.
    - The machine homes to the back right corner.
    - The machine then asks to calibrate the tool height.

## Program Setup

To begin, load the G-code file from a flash drive prepared by the Fusion programmer.

1. Open the file loader in Carbide Motion.
2. Navigate to the G-code file on the right side.
3. Open the file.
4. Click **Top View** and check the part orientation and origin.
5. Go to the **Jog** menu.
6. Position the spindle above the desired origin point.
7. Click **Set Zero X & Y** and confirm the origin point is set.

:::note
Only set Z zero if you believe the height is off.
:::

## Securing Material

1. Place the material on the bed.
2. Use the green clamps to hold the material in place.
3. Make sure the material is secure and will not move during operation.

:::warning
Material that is not secured can damage the machine and injure the operator.
:::
