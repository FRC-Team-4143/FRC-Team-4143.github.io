Every team member must understand these rules before using any equipment. Each machine page also has its own safety
section covering hazards specific to that machine.
:::

## Personal Protective Equipment

### Loose Clothing

All clothing, including pants, shirts, jackets, and sweatshirts, must fit closely to the body and not hang loosely into
work areas. **Pay particular attention to drawstrings**, which can pose a serious hazard if left unsecured.

### Closed-Toe Shoes

**Closed-toe shoes are required at all times in the engineering lab.** Open-toe footwear, including sandals, flip-flops,
and clogs, is strictly prohibited due to the risk of hot chips, falling objects, and other physical and chemical
hazards.

### Jewelry

**Jewelry can pose a serious safety hazard.** Remove rings, unsecured necklaces, loose bracelets, and watches before
machining. They can get caught in machinery or conduct electricity.

### Safety Glasses

Safety glasses must be worn whenever work is being performed nearby, to protect against flying debris. **Regular
glasses are not a suitable replacement for safety glasses.**

### Long Hair

**Long hair must be securely restrained** before entering a work area. Hair longer than shoulder length must be tied
back or secured with a cap or bandanna to prevent entanglement in machinery. Loose ponytails or unsecured strands are
not acceptable.

## Behavior

- **No horseplay.** Pranks, physical contact, and distractions can lead to serious injuries and are strictly forbidden
  in the engineering lab.
- **Avoid distractions.** While actively machining, do not use mobile devices or distract others.

## Housekeeping

- **Clean debris.** Remove chips and debris from floors, machines, and work tables regularly using brushes or tools,
  never bare hands.
- **Store tools.** Don't leave tools on machines. Return them to their designated storage areas.
- **Clean spills.** Clean spills immediately to prevent slips and contamination.
