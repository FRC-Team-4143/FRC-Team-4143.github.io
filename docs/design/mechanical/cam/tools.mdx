## Choosing a Tool

We mostly use **drills** and **endmills**. Look at the ends: a drill comes to a point, and an endmill is flat (or
rounded, for a ball endmill) so it can cut sideways.

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| 0.159" drill | 10-32 tap hole |
| 0.201" drill | 1/4-20 tap hole, and 10-32 bolt clearance hole |
| 1/8" endmill | Can be weak. Works well on polycarbonate. |
| 1/4" endmill | Perfect for the Tormach. Cut less than 3/8" per pass. Use it to bore larger holes, like 1.125" bearing bores. |

- Holes are easy to make bigger with a hand drill.
- Peck drill if the material is thicker than 0.25".
- Bigger machines can use bigger tools.

## CNC Mill Work Offsets

:::caution Needs confirmation
These work offset locations have not all been confirmed yet. Double check on the machine before running a program.
:::

| Fusion ID | G-Code ID | Location |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | G54 | Right side, bottom of vise. |
| 2 | G55 | Left side, bottom of vise. |
| 3 | G56 | Right side, bottom of vise +0.5x. |
| 4 | **G57** ⭐ | Left side, top of parallels in vise. |
| 5 | G58 | Right side, top of parallels in vise. |
| 6 | G59 | Back right, outside vise hooks -3x. |

:::note
- All work offsets are relative to the back face of the vise, not the front face.
- ⭐ **G57** is the most commonly used work offset in our shop.
:::
