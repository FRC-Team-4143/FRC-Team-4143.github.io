Autodesk Fusion is free for students and educators. Follow these steps to get access and join the team's shared
workspace.

### 1. Go to Autodesk's Education Portal

Visit the [Autodesk Education Software page](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/fusion), or go directly to
[Student Access](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/overview?utm_source=ec_stu_us&utm_medium=int_ref&utm_campaign=edumkt_eg#FSN).

### 2. Create an Autodesk Account

Click **Get Started**, or **Sign In** if you already have an account.

:::tip
Use your school email address if possible. It helps with verification.
:::

### 3. Verify Your Educational Status

- Select your role: **Student**, or **Mentor** if you're a mentor working with a FIRST team.
- Provide your school or organization name and expected graduation date.

### 4. Join the MARS/WARS Project Team

Send Mr. Shoff a Slack message with your Autodesk account email and ask to be added to the project team.
