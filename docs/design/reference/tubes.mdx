Tubes are long, so orientation matters: the robot has to pick them up from any angle and place them pointing the right
way. Robots that handle them well tend to have:

- **A ground intake that funnels the tube into one orientation**, often handing off through an indexer.
- **A wristed end effector** that can score at several angles and heights.
- **A fast elevator or arm**, kept light by moving weight out of the end effector.

Behind the Bumpers didn't exist yet for this game, so use reveal videos, Chief Delphi threads, and team pages.

Containers went on top of tote stacks ([Boxes](./boxes.mdx#frc-2015-recycle-rush-totes)), and the four in the middle of the field were fought over in the first seconds of the match with fast "can burglar" arms. Litter (green pool noodles, about 2.6 in across and almost 5 ft long) went inside the containers.
