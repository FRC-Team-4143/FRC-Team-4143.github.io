Large balls are usually handled one at a time. Robots that handle them well tend to have:

- **Compliant wheels or grippy rollers** that squeeze the ball. Compression and grip matter more than speed.
- **A grabber that holds the ball firmly** while the robot drives and gets defended.
- **One end effector for two game pieces** when a game mixes balls with something else (2019 and 2025).
