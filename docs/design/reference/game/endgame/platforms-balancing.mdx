Some endgames ask robots to get on top of something instead of hanging from it: a raised platform, a ramp, or a
platform that tips. Robots that do this well tend to have:

- **A low, strong drivetrain** and bumpers that clear the edge of the platform.
- **A way to lift the whole robot up a step**, such as legs or wheels that push down while the drivetrain drives
  forward.
- **Good driving or automatic balancing code** for tilting platforms, often using the gyro.

Engaged means the station is level with every robot on it docked. Balancing three robots at once was common in
playoffs. Most top teams balanced automatically with the gyro in auto.

Getting a whole robot up a 19 in step took a lot of power. Common designs were legs that pushed the back of the robot
up while a small wheel drove it forward, or a lifter that pulled the front up onto the platform. Some robots lifted
partners onto Level 2 or 3.

## FRC 2012 Rebound Rumble (bridges)

Robots drove onto tipping bridges and balanced them. One robot scored 10 points and two or three scored 20 (more in
playoffs). A white bridge in the middle paired robots from opposite alliances for ranking credit.

## FRC 2007 Rack 'N' Roll (lifting partners)

The whole endgame was a buddy lift. A robot raised at least 4 in off the carpet by a partner scored 15 points, and at
least 12 in scored 30. Robots carried fold-out ramps or lifting wings.

| Team | What to look at | Links |
|---|---|---|
| 254 Cheesy Poofs, "Raptor" | Two fold-out ramps that lifted partners over 12 in. | [Season page](https://www.team254.com/first/2007/) |
| 190 Gompei | Wings that lowered for partners, then lifted them 1 ft. | [Robot page](https://wp.wpi.edu/frc190/?p=53) |

## FRC 2006 Aim High (ramp)

Robots drove up a ramp onto their alliance platform. One robot scored 5 points, two scored 10, and three scored 25.

:::info FTC
FTC (FIRST Tech Challenge) robots are smaller, but they climb and balance the same ways. FTC teams share engineering portfolios and CAD instead of Behind the Bumpers videos. The [gm0 robot gallery](https://gm0.org/en/latest/docs/appendix/gallery.html) lists them by season.
:::

## FTC 2025-26 DECODE (base)

Both robots end the match in an 18 in square base. Two robots can only fit if one lifts the other or climbs onto it,
so the endgame is a lift: one robot pulls its partner up, or raises itself onto its partner.

## FTC 2017-18 Relic Recovery (balancing stone)

Robots ended the match balanced on their low balancing stone, the same platform they started on.
