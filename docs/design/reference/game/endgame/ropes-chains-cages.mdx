A rope, chain, or hanging cage moves when the robot touches it, so it is harder to grab than a fixed bar. Robots that
climb them well tend to have:

- **A grabber that centers itself** on a moving target: a funnel, a V-shaped hook, or a clamp that closes on contact.
- **Something that braces against the structure** (rollers or a bumper) so the robot doesn't swing or twist as it
  lifts.
- **A fast winch or elevator that holds with the power off.**

A deep cage hangs almost to the carpet, so there is no room to drive under it. The robot has to grab the cage, then
lift itself and the cage much higher. Cages swing, so the grabber has to catch a moving target. An early Team Update
gave different cage heights, so check the final manual.

at the same time.

many used a knotted rope that their spool gripped better.
