# Endgame & Climbing

Most games end with a task worth a lot of points in the last 15 to 30 seconds: hang from a bar, climb a rope or chain,
or balance on a platform. When a new game is released, find past endgames with a similar structure and study the robots
that climbed fastest and most reliably.

2005, 2008, 2009, 2014, and 2015 had no climbing or balancing endgame.

## What to Look For

- **How fast is the climb?** Top robots climb in a few seconds, so they can keep scoring longer. Count how many seconds
  each robot spends on the endgame.
- **How does it hold on after the buzzer?** Scoring is judged after the match ends, so the robot has to stay up with
  the power off. Look for the ratchet, brake, or latch.
- **Does it help partners?** Some games rewarded lifting or hanging partners (buddy climbs); others ignored it.
- **How does it line up?** A funnel, a wide hook, or a camera that lines up the robot saves seconds every match.

The [Climbers](../../mechanisms/climbers.mdx) page covers how climbing mechanisms work and which robots to study.

:::caution
Dimensions on these pages come from game manuals and Team Updates, which change during a season. Check the final
manual for the year before designing to a number.
:::
