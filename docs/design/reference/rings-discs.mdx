Flat game pieces lie low on the floor, so the intake has to get under or around them. Robots that handle them well
tend to have:

- **An under-the-bumper or full-width ground intake** that grabs the piece from any direction.
- **A feeder or indexer** that centers the piece and hands it to the shooter consistently.
- **Rollers or flywheels geared together** so the piece leaves the shooter the same way every time.
- **A grabber or suction cup** when the piece is placed instead of shot (2019 hatch panels).
