Small balls are usually collected in large numbers and shot into a goal. Robots that handle them well tend to have:

- **A full-width ground intake** that can be deployed outside the frame perimeter. "Touch it, keep it" is the goal.
- **An indexer** (a hopper, serializer, or spindexer) that lines balls up single file and feeds them to the shooter
  one at a time without jamming.
- **A flywheel shooter with an adjustable hood**, often on a **turret** so the robot can shoot while driving.

:::tip Single file or full width?
604's 2026 prototypes fed about 15 balls/s single file versus about 23 balls/s full width. They chose a single-file
turret anyway, so they could shoot while moving. Decide what your robot needs before picking an indexer.
:::
