Cones and tetras are tall and pointed, so they tip over and can be picked up in more than one orientation. Robots that
handle them well tend to have:

- **A gripper that self-centers the piece**, whether it is standing up or lying on its side.
- **A ground intake separate from a light gripper** on the elevator or arm, so the lift moves faster.
- **A fast elevator or a well-counterbalanced arm** to reach every scoring height.

Behind the Bumpers didn't exist yet for this game, so use reveal videos, Chief Delphi threads, and team pages.

Robots grabbed tetras by the top corner and stacked them onto goals, so a hook or claw on a tall arm was the common design.
